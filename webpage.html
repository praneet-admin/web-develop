About Us

Chennai's Trusted PEB & Structural Fabrication Firm

SPAT Construction is an established engineering and contracting firm based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. We specialize in Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB), structural steel fabrication, and roof cladding solutions for industrial, commercial, and infrastructure clients across the region.

Every project we undertake is driven by four core commitments: engineering precision, high-grade steel and materials, strict safety compliance, and on-schedule delivery. From design to erection, we manage the full project lifecycle with expertise.

What We Do

Our Services

From PEB design and fabrication to roofing and erection, we handle every stage of your steel structure project.

🏗️

Pre-Engineered Buildings (PEB)

Factory-fabricated steel building systems designed for fast on-site erection — ideal for warehouses, factories, and large commercial spans.

⚙️

Structural Fabrication

Custom steel structural components — columns, beams, trusses, and frames — fabricated to engineering drawings with precision and quality control.

🔩

Roof Cladding

Durable roofing and wall cladding using colour-coated and galvanized steel sheets, providing weatherproof and long-lasting building envelopes.

🏢

Erection & Contracting

End-to-end structural erection services — from foundation anchor bolts to final bolt tightening — managed by experienced site engineers.
